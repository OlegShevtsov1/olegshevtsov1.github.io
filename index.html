03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE TABS

PERSONAL NOTE / FROM COMPUTER ENGINEERING TO AGENTS

I studied computer engineering (M.Sc., Khmelnytskyi National University) and came to Ruby on Rails in 2019. Since then I've worked on systems where mistakes are expensive: a national UK NHS dental triage service that routed 100K+ referrals, an IoT platform at FluentPet, and a crypto exchange.

At ManageBac — a learning platform used by 3,000+ IB schools — I built the backend of the AI assistant chat, prompt chips and token-usage analytics in a 10+-year Rails monolith. Along the way: pentest findings fixed across 3 vulnerability classes, test coverage up 40%, p90 query latency down 15%.

Now I build Pilot end to end with my own agentic SDLC toolkit. My favorite part hasn't changed: ask why first, reuse what already works, and only call it done when the evidence says so.
